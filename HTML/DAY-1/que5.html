Gourmet Delights

Appetizers

Appetizers are delicious small dishes served before the main course to stimulate your appetite. They are perfect for starting a meal with tasty bites.


Main Courses

Main Course is the main part of a meal, offering delicious and filling dishes prepared with fresh ingredients and rich flavors to satisfy your hunger.


Desserts

Desserts are sweet treats served at the end of a meal, including cakes, ice creams, pastries, and other delicious delights that make every meal special.

Paneer Tikka

Paneer tikka is a popular North Indian grilled appetizer made by marinating cubes of paneer (Indian fresh cheese) with yogurt, spices, and vegetables before roasting them, traditionally in a tandoor. It is celebrated for its smoky flavor, vibrant seasoning, and widespread popularity in Indian restaurants around the world.

Paneer Lababdar

Paneer Lababdar is a rich North Indian curry made with paneer (Indian cottage cheese) cooked in a creamy, tomato-based gravy. It is especially known for its smooth texture, buttery flavor, and mildly sweet, aromatic sauce, making it a popular choice in Indian restaurants and festive meals.

Malai Kofta

Malai kofta (मलाई कोफ़्ता) is a rich North Indian vegetarian dish of fried dumplings—typically made from paneer and potato—served in a smooth, creamy gravy. It is associated with North Indian and Mughlai-style cooking and is especially known as an indulgent restaurant, wedding, and celebration dish.

Shahi Paneer

Shahi paneer (शाही पनीर) is a rich, creamy North Indian vegetarian curry featuring soft cubes of paneer (fresh Indian cheese) in a smooth, aromatic gravy. Associated with the royal culinary traditions of Mughlai cuisine, it is known for its luxurious texture, mild spices, and delicate sweetness. It is particularly popular at Indian restaurants, weddings, and festive meals.

Dal Makhani

Dal Makhani is a rich, slow-cooked lentil dish from the Punjab region of the Indian subcontinent. Made primarily with whole black gram (urad dal) and kidney beans, it is celebrated for its creamy texture, deep flavor, and status as one of the signature dishes of North Indian cuisine.
